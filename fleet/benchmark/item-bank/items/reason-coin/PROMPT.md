# Task: a parity reasoning problem

You have 30 coins in a row, all heads-up. You flip every second coin
(coins 2, 4, 6, ..., 30). Then you flip every third coin (3, 6, 9,
..., 30). At the end, how many coins are heads-up, and which positions
are they?

Write your answer to `answer.txt` as a comma-separated list of the
positions (1-indexed) that are heads-up at the end, and on a final
line state the COUNT.

Example format:
```
positions: 2, 4, 6
count: 3
```
