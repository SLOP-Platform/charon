# Task: a small numerical reasoning problem

A positive integer is divisible by both 6 and 15. What is the smallest
such integer that is strictly greater than 100?

Reply with ONLY the integer digits, no words, no punctuation, no
explanation. The grader extracts the first run of digits from your
response and compares it to the expected answer.

Hint: a number divisible by both 6 and 15 is divisible by lcm(6, 15).
