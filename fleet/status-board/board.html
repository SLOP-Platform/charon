How to read this page

There are three verdicts, never two. The third one is the important one.

✓PASSING
We ran the check, it passed, and someone has demonstrated that this check is capable of failing. Only then is “it passed” worth anything.
✗FAILING
We ran the check and it failed. A failure is always trustworthy, because we watched it happen.
?UNPROVEN
We cannot tell you. Either the check would not run, or nothing has ever shown that the check is able to notice a problem — so “it passed” would mean nothing. This is deliberately not shown as good news. A page that turns everything green is a page that lies.
•COUNTED
Not a verdict at all — just a headcount, in its own section at the bottom. There is no right or wrong number for these, so no health claim is made. Counted is not the same as passing.

Three limits apply to every tile above, including the green ones. First, this is a snapshot: it says nothing about what happened five minutes after it was taken. Second, a passing check only ever covers what someone thought to check — there is no tile for “the problem nobody has imagined yet”. Third, this page reports what the detectors say; it does not re-derive their numbers, so a detector with a blind spot is a blind spot here too.
